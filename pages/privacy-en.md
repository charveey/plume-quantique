### Summary

- We use services like **Netlify**, **Welcomments**, **DecapCMS**, **Formspree**, **Google Analytics**, and **Microsoft Clarity**.
- No personal data is sent to DecapCMS. Netlify may see visitor activity (IP, pages, etc.).
- Google Analytics is only activated after cookie consent; IPs are anonymized.
- Microsoft Clarity provides anonymized insights for site optimization, security, and advertising.
- Formspree handles contact form submissions.
- Welcomments stores pre-rendered static comments; no personal data is collected without consent.
- You have GDPR rights over personal data (though this site does not actively collect any).
- Essential cookies are always enabled; non-essential cookies require consent.
- Questions? Contact **contact@le7eptiemeoeil.com**.

### How we process your data

This website is powered by **Jekyll**, **Welcomments**, **DecapCMS**, **Formspree**, and hosted on **Netlify**.

- **DecapCMS** is only used as an external CMS. No traffic or visitor data ends up at DecapCMS or their partners.
- **Netlify**, like any hosting company, can access visitor data such as IP addresses and visited pages. Note that Netlify is not known to actively profile visitors. Using a VPN can help limit this visibility.

### Google Analytics

This website uses **Google Analytics** only if you approve third-party cookies and scripts.

We have taken measures to limit Google’s data collection:

- IP addresses are anonymized
- SSL is enforced at all times

### Microsoft Clarity

**Microsoft Clarity** provides anonymized data only, which is not stored by us.

**Microsoft Clarity** capture how you interact with this website through:

- Behavioral metrics
- Heatmaps
- Session replay

Data is collected using first- and third-party cookies and other tracking technologies to monitor online activity.

We use this information for:

- Site optimization
- Fraud and security purposes

For more details about Microsoft’s data collection and usage, please visit the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://www.microsoft.com/privacy/privacystatement/).

### Formspree

**Formspree** is used to handle the contact form submissions on this website.

- When you submit a message, your name, email, and message content are sent directly to Formspree.
- No other data from your visit is shared with Formspree.
- Formspree may process this data to deliver emails to the site owner and comply with their security policies.

For more details, see [Formspree Privacy Policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

### Welcomments

**Welcomments** is used to handle comments on this website.

- Comments are pre-rendered into static HTML for performance and privacy.
- No personal data is collected without your consent.
- Commenters may choose to provide a name, email, or website, which is stored by Welcomments.
- You can review Welcomments' data handling at [Welcomments Privacy Policy](https://welcomments.io/privacy).

### Your Privacy Rights (EU/EEA/UK/Switzerland)

In accordance with GDPR and other applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data (note: this website does not actively collect personal data):

1. To be informed about the collection and use of your personal data
2. To access the personal data we hold about you
3. To request correction or deletion of your personal data
4. To restrict or object to the processing of your personal data
5. To withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
6. To lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe your data protection rights have been violated

### Cookie Consent

This website uses a **cookie consent banner** to comply with EU/EEA/UK/Swiss law.

- Non-essential cookies (including those used by Microsoft Clarity) are set **only after you provide explicit consent**.
- You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the cookie settings link at the bottom of the page.
- **Essential cookies**, necessary for the operation of the website, are always enabled.

### Data Sharing and Transfers

Data collected via Microsoft Clarity may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including the United States.

Microsoft claims to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable law. We have no direct control over these measures.
